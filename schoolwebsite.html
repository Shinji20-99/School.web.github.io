About School

Jose C. Payumo Jr. Memorial High School is a public secondary school located in Barangay Naparing, Dinalupihan, Bataan. Since its establishment, the school has been committed to providing quality education and developing responsible, competent, and lifelong learners.

The school offers Junior High School and Senior High School programs, including academic strands such as STEM, HUMSS, and GAS, as well as Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) specializations. Through dedicated teachers and supportive stakeholders, the school continues to empower students with knowledge, skills, and values necessary for their future success.

The school aims to create a safe, inclusive, and learner-centered environment that promotes academic excellence, leadership, and community involvement.

Mission

To provide accessible, quality, and learner-centered education that develops responsible, competent, and productive learners equipped with knowledge, skills, and values for lifelong success.

Vision

A leading educational institution that nurtures academically excellent, socially responsible, and globally competitive learners who contribute positively to society.
